# Puerto Rico political economy

The main passion behind most of my work. My frame is that research should show the right ways to look for solutions rather than hand down the solutions themselves. I've seen economics as a tool to change politics since high school.

## Where it shows up
- [[PROMESA Section 204 count]] tracks how often the oversight board has overridden the elected government.
- [[Proyecto Esencia and Act 60]] is about tax incentives, development and who carries the cost.
- [[Marriage collapse in Puerto Rico]] looks for an institutional mechanism behind a demographic shift.
- [[FDI in the Caribbean]] puts Puerto Rico next to the Dominican Republic and Jamaica.
- [[San Juan transit]] and [[Education policy in Puerto Rico]] are the more local, applied pieces.
- [[Appalachia Volunteers in Las Marías]] and [[PRSA]] are where the island shows up outside research.
- Puerto Rico clients at work (BGCPR, Vimenti, PSI) run through [[CAP grant work]] and [[CAP newsletters]].
