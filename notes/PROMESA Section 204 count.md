# PROMESA Section 204 count

## What it is
A complete count of laws from Puerto Rico's legislature that the federal oversight board blocked, nullified or sued to void under PROMESA Section 204.

## The twist
I split the count by whether the elected government spent money on litigation to defend the law. Paying to defend a law is a costly signal that the government meant it.

## What I claim
Only the count is new. I'm not overselling past that.
