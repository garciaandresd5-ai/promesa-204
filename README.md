# PROMESA Section 204 count

A complete count of laws passed by Puerto Rico's legislature that the Financial Oversight and Management Board blocked, nullified or sued to void under PROMESA Section 204.

The count is split by whether the elected government spent money in court to defend each law. Paying to defend a law is a costly signal that the government meant it. Only the count is new; the project does not claim more than that.

## What is here

**data/** holds the count itself, `conteo-junta-17-leyes.csv` and the same records as JSON. Each of the 17 rows is one law, with its subject and the board's action in Spanish and English, the action and resolution dates, days to resolution, whether the case is still open, the outcome, the sector and a source citation. As of the last update, 11 cases are closed and six from 2024 are still open.

**notes/** holds the project note and the related theme notes from my Obsidian vault. The `[[double bracket]]` links point to other notes in the vault.

**drafts/** holds the Substack essay "What Acemoglu gets right about Puerto Rico without mentioning it once," which draws on this work. English versions run from an early draft through v7, and the Spanish versions are in their own folder. All drafts were converted from Word to Markdown.

## What is not here

The source literature (Skeel, the Puerto Rico law review articles, Weaver, Groseclose, Putnam and the rest) lives in Google Drive, not in this repo, because those works are copyrighted.

## Keeping it current

The working copies of the data and notes are on my Mac in Desktop/GW and the vault. When either changes, copy the new version here and commit it, so the history records every change to how a law was coded.
